PROPOSED CASE STUDYPRODUCT DISCOVERY

Making payment status easier to understand

Small businesses and individuals can lose time when payment records are scattered or statuses are unclear. This concept explores how a simple tracker could improve visibility.

USER PROBLEM

Users need a quick way to know what is pending, completed, or overdue without manually checking every record.

HYPOTHESIS

A consistent status field, due date, and searchable list may reduce the effort needed to review payments.

MVP SCOPE
  • Payment status and due date
  • Search and status filters
  • Basic overdue indicator
SUCCESS METRICS TO TEST
Time to find a record · Status accuracy · Weekly active usage
Next step: validate the problem with users before building.
